Latihan PTS Bahasa Inggris - Kelas 1

📚 Ringkasan Materi Pembelajaran

1. Greetings & Partings (Salam dan Perpisahan)

  • Good morning = Selamat pagi (Diucapkan saat matahari terbit / pagi hari)
  • Good afternoon = Selamat siang/sore
  • Good evening = Selamat malam
  • Good night = Selamat malam (Diucapkan saat akan tidur atau berpisah di malam hari)
  • How are you today? = Bagaimana kabarmu? → Jawaban: I am fine (Saya baik-baik saja)
  • What is your name? = Siapa namamu? → Jawaban: My name is... (Nama saya adalah...)
  • Nice to meet you = Senang bertemu denganmu → Jawaban: Nice to meet you too (Senang bertemu denganmu juga)
  • Thank you = Terima kasih → Jawaban: You are welcome (Sama-sama)
  • Good bye / See you = Selamat tinggal / Sampai jumpa

2. Numbers (Angka 0-10)

  • 0 = Zero
  • 1 = One
  • 2 = Two
  • 3 = Three
  • 4 = Four
  • 5 = Five
  • 6 = Six
  • 7 = Seven
  • 8 = Eight
  • 9 = Nine
  • 10 = Ten

3. Classroom Objects (Benda-benda di Sekolah)

  • Eraser = Penghapus
  • Sharpener = Serutan pensil
  • Pencil = Pensil
  • Pen = Pulpen
  • Book = Buku
  • Bag = Tas
  • Hat = Topi
  • Ruler = Penggaris
  • Table = Meja
  • Chair = Kursi
