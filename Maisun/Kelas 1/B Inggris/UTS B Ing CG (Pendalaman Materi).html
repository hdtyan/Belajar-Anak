📚 Materi Sebelum Mengerjakan Soal

1. Greetings (Salam / Sapaan)

Good morning = selamat pagi • Good afternoon = selamat siang/sore • Good evening = selamat sore/malam • Good night = selamat malam • Hello / Hi = halo / hai

2. Introducing Yourself & Asking Names

What is your name? = siapa namamu? • My name is … = nama saya … • How are you today? = bagaimana kabarmu hari ini? • I am fine = saya baik-baik saja

3. Nice to Meet You

Nice to meet you. = senang bertemu denganmu. • Nice to meet you too. = senang bertemu denganmu juga.

4. Thank You & You Are Welcome

Thank you. = terima kasih. • You are welcome. = sama-sama.

5. Farewells (Berpamitan)

Good bye = selamat tinggal. • See you. = sampai jumpa. • See you too. = sampai jumpa juga.

6. Numbers 0–10

0 zero • 1 one • 2 two • 3 three • 4 four • 5 five • 6 six • 7 seven • 8 eight • 9 nine • 10 ten

7. Numbers & Classroom Objects

Contoh dari latihan: I have five sharpeners. • Riri has nine erasers. • Rasya has six hats.
